# SENAI Visual Identity
Philosophy: The brand is the most valuable asset. Consistency builds authority.
Core Principle: Follow the 2021 Revision strictly, but prioritize accessibility and legibility in digital contexts.

🎯 Selective Reading Rule (MANDATORY)
Section Status When to Consult

Color System & Contrast 🔴 REQUIRED Starting any new layout or UI component
Brand Architecture 🔴 REQUIRED Using FIESP, SESI, and SENAI together
Typography & Spacing ⚪ Optional Detailed document design or long-form copy
Motion & Effects ⚪ Optional Applying the brand to video or web interactions

## 1. Decision Tree: Brand Application
Where is the brand being applied?

- White Background (Preferred)
  - Use "Vermelho SENAI" (#e30613) - Maximum balance and clarity.

- Colored or Dark Background
  - Is the background the same shade of red as the brand?
    - YES: Use White (Negative) application.
    - NO: Check contrast ratio (WCAG compliance)
      - High Contrast: Use Vermelho SENAI.
      - Low Contrast: Use Monochromatic (White or Black).

- Digital Environment (Web/App)
  - Is the logo height less than 14px?
    - YES: Increase size (Technical legibility limit).
    - NO: Standard application allowed.

- Multi-Entity Layout (Horizontal Architecture)
  - Follow Priority: 1.FIESP > 2.CIESP > 3.SESI > 4.SENAI > 5.IRS

## 3. UX & Visual Principles (Manual Adaptation)
**Clearance Area (Non-Interference)**
- Rule: The minimum "breathing space" around the logo must be equal to the distance from the letter "i" to the right edge of the logo.
- Psychology: Reduces visual noise and ensures the brand remains the focal point (Figure/Ground principle).

**Hierarchy & Grouping (Miller's Law)**
- Architecture: When grouping entities, the SENAI logo must always follow SESI in a horizontal line to respect the institutional hierarchy.
- Legibility: Never use the brand smaller than 5mm in print to avoid cognitive strain.

## 4. Compliance Checklist
Before finalizing any asset, run this audit:
- [ ] Entity Priority: If SESI and SENAI are present, does SESI come first?
- [ ] Contrast Check: Is the logo legible over the chosen background image/color?
- [ ] Breathing Space: Is the non-interference zone respected on all sides?
- [ ] Color Profile: Are you using CMYK for print and RGB/Hex for web?
- [ ] Integrity: Is the logo free from shadows, gradients, or distortions?

## 5. Visual Anti-Patterns (DO NOT DO)
- ❌ Distortion: Stretching or compressing the logo.
- ❌ Unauthorized Colors: Using "Red" that isn't Pantone 485.
- ❌ Effects: Applying drop shadows, glows, or outlines (Keep it flat).
- ❌ Sequence Error: Placing SENAI before FIESP or SESI in joint applications.
